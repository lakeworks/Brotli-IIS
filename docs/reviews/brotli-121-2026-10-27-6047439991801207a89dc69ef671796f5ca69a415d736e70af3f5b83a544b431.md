### [O1] Keep worker OOM in the truncation diagnosis

[CLAUDE.md:61](/D:/CC/INTERNAL/Brotli-IIS/.claude/worktrees/brotli-121/CLAUDE.md:61) tells operators to look for worker termination “not for truncation,” then prioritizes intermediaries.

Those symptoms are not mutually exclusive. Brotli can emit output before finishing and subsequently allocate again (`c/enc/encode.c:1061–1071,1658–1689`). The default allocator terminates the process on failure (`c/enc/memory.c:48–50`). **If response bytes have already been sent, that termination can leave an incomplete compressed response.** It also interrupts other responses in the worker.

Keep the correction about small output buffers, but explicitly include origin OOM/worker termination in truncation triage. Qualify the blanket advice against lowering quality: it cannot repair buffer-draining logic, but reducing allocation pressure can mitigate an established OOM cause.
